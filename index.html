Campus Alert

It started with one suspiciously perfect pie in the student lounge. Now the halls are full of snack-seeking stragglers, and nobody can agree how many slices are left. Stay calm, keep a little distance, and do not follow anyone carrying a suspiciously large fork.

Three Survival Rules

  1. Trust the label, not the smell.

    Check the date and ingredients before eating unmarked food. A mystery snack is not worth the risk.

  2. Keep your hands clean.

    Wash your hands after touching shared doors, desks, or snack tables. It is a simple way to help avoid getting sick.

  3. Move with a buddy.

    Stay with someone you trust and use familiar, well-lit routes. Getting lost is a problem you can avoid.

Survival Kit

Water bottle

Bring water so you can stay hydrated if you need to take a long detour.

First-aid supplies

Bandages and wipes can help with small scrapes while you find a safe place.

Sealed trail mix

A snack that does not need refrigeration can help when the cafeteria is crowded.

The Safe Zone

The Library Quiet Floor

Meet your buddy at the main entrance and check that the route is clear before going inside. Choose a staffed study area and keep exits accessible. Let someone you trust know where you are. If the building feels unsafe, leave and contact campus security or emergency services.
